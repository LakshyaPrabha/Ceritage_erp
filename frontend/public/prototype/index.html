ℹ️ BIS Hallmarking — Key Rules
📅 Mandatory Since
BIS Hallmarking is mandatory for gold jewelry above 2 grams since 16 June 2021.
🔢 HUID Format
6-character alphanumeric code. E.g. HM2026A001. Unique per piece.
⚖️ Purity Marks
24K = 999 · 22K = 916 · 18K = 750 · 14K = 585
🔍 Verify Online
BIS Caremark App or bis.gov.in portal to verify any HUID instantly.
📋 Exemptions
Silver, platinum, and gold items below 2g are currently exempt from mandatory hallmarking.
⚠️ Penalty
Selling non-hallmarked gold (covered items) can attract fine up to ₹1 lakh or 1 year imprisonment.
